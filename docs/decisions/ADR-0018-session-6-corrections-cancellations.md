## Decision (the Owner's rulings, refinements incorporated)

1. **S6-D1 — Posted documents are immutable.** A Posted financial document is
   never edited and never deleted; it is preserved permanently. An error is
   handled by cancellation (never by altering the document), then a new correct
   document if needed. Governing goal: a complete audit trail — what happened,
   who did it, when, and which document corrected the error.
2. **S6-D2 — A permitted cancellation reverses all effects automatically
   (approved wording).** *If cancellation is permitted, all financial effects
   generated by that document are automatically reversed and the system returns
   to the state immediately before that document existed.* (Worded to hold
   independently of the dependency rule S6-D3, which forbids cancellation when
   dependents exist.) The reversal updates Cash Balance, teacher entitlement,
   Center Net Balance, and all derived balances and reports with no further user
   action.
3. **S6-D3 — Dependency rule (approved wording — the term "LIFO" is deliberately
   not used anywhere).** *A document cannot be cancelled while later financial
   documents depend on it. Dependent documents must be removed beginning with the
   newest dependent document until the original document becomes independent.*
   Examples: a teacher Payment Voucher must be cancelled before the receipt it
   drew from; a Refund Voucher must be cancelled before the receipt it depends
   on. Cancellation never creates automatic debts or open items — the user
   removes dependents first.
4. **S6-D4 — Cancellation representation.** No separate cancellation document is
   created. The original keeps its place and carries a **"Cancelled"** status —
   never deleted, never hidden — and remains visible in the voucher log, the
   student (member) statement, financial-history reports, and the activity
   timeline, clearly shown as cancelled. Each cancellation records its date, its
   reason (mandatory), and who cancelled it.
5. **S6-D5 — Financial vs descriptive fields.** Financial fields — amount,
   student, program, payment method, or anything affecting balances,
   entitlements, or financial reports — are never edited after posting;
   correction is always cancel + create a new correct document. Descriptive
   fields — notes, Payer Name, extra description, anything with no financial
   effect — may be edited after posting, provided the change is recorded in the
   activity log with date, user, and old value → new value; such an edit
   triggers no financial recalculation.
6. **S6-D6 — No Draft stage in V1.** Saving a financial voucher IS posting it:
   every financial document becomes Posted immediately on save. Version 1 has no
   `Draft → Review → Posted` lifecycle. A Draft stage is a possible future
   development only, recorded as a Future Consideration — not an active V1 rule.
7. **S6-D7 — Model complete for V1.** No additional known exceptional
   situations. Future rare cases will be documented, studied, and — if warranted
   — added via a new ADR in a later version, never pre-emptively.

## Interpretation boundaries (what is NOT decided here)

- **Actor identity:** recording "who cancelled" resolves trivially to the single
  owner (F-02); no user-account/authentication concept is introduced, and no new
  unknown arises.
- **Cancellation as a timeline event:** a cancellation is a meaningful business
  activity and therefore appears on the Operations activity timeline (DR-018,
  DR-019) as an append-only event — consistent, not a new rule.
- **Descriptive-field edits and the timeline:** logged descriptive edits are
  likewise activity-timeline events (DR-019); the exact field-level list of
  "descriptive vs financial" beyond the named fields is a Product/DDL-phase
  detail, not a domain gap.
