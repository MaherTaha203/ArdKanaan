## 1. Purpose

This document defines the complete specification of the Receipt Voucher under ARK-000, ARK-001, and ARK-002. It defines everything required for the Receipt Voucher as a product object. It does not define implementation, database tables, UI layout, or APIs.

## 2. Scope

This document defines only the Receipt Voucher as a product object, including its business purpose, when it exists, what information it captures, what information it generates, and its relationship with the Student, Student Statement, Course, and Financial Truth.

This document does not define implementation, database schema, SQL, APIs, screen layout, button positions, UI styling, field validation logic, calculations, or the numbering algorithm.

## 3. Business Purpose

The Receipt Voucher exists to create incoming financial facts.

It exists because incoming money must exist inside Ard Kanaan only through voucher truth.

The Receipt Voucher is a financial document of the product, not a statement, not a report, and not academic course management.

## 4. When A Receipt Voucher Exists

Every payment creates a Receipt Voucher.

Every Receipt Voucher represents exactly one financial event.

A Receipt Voucher exists whenever incoming money is created inside the product.

## 5. Information Captured By A Receipt Voucher

The Receipt Voucher captures the fact that a payment occurred.

The Receipt Voucher captures the Student to whom the payment belongs.

The Receipt Voucher captures the Course as financial context only.

The Receipt Voucher captures the course value as human-entered financial context.

The Receipt Voucher captures only facts, not derived results.

## 6. Information Generated By A Receipt Voucher

The Receipt Voucher generates incoming financial facts.

The Receipt Voucher generates financial truth because vouchers are the sole financial source of truth.

The Receipt Voucher generates financial information that may later be presented by the Student Statement and the Financial Report.

## 7. Relationship With Student

The Student is the central business entity of the product.

The Receipt Voucher belongs to the Student's financial history.

If the Receipt Voucher is the first financial transaction, it creates the Student.

Future payments reuse the existing Student.

## 8. Relationship With Student Statement

The Student Statement is generated automatically.

The Student Statement is never edited manually.

The Receipt Voucher contributes financial facts to the Student Statement.

All obligations belong to the same Student Statement.

The Receipt Voucher does not become the Student Statement.

## 9. Relationship With Course

The Course exists only as financial context.

The Receipt Voucher may carry Course financial context.

A Student may have financial obligations in multiple Courses.

A new Course creates a new financial obligation inside the existing Student Statement.

The Receipt Voucher does not turn the Course into an academic-management object.

## 10. Relationship With Financial Truth

The Receipt Voucher is a source of financial truth.

The Receipt Voucher is not a presentation object.

The Receipt Voucher is not derived information.

The Receipt Voucher creates financial facts that later derived information may present.

## 11. Traceability

Every financial value displayed by the system must always be traceable back to the originating Receipt Voucher.

The Receipt Voucher is therefore a traceable origin of financial truth.

## 12. Editing Rules

Receipt Vouchers may be corrected only by correcting the voucher itself.

Receipt Voucher correction never happens by editing the Student Statement.

## 13. Printing

Printing is supported for the Receipt Voucher.

This document does not define print layout or print format.
