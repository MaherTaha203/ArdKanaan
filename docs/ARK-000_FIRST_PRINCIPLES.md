## 1. Purpose

This document records the immutable first principles of Ard Kanaan exactly as approved by the Owner. It is the highest product authority of the reset documentation set. It defines product nature, product boundaries, financial truth, statement truth, correction truth, traceability, and simplicity. It is not a specification, implementation guidance, UI guidance, or database design.

## 2. Scope

This document contains only Owner-approved product philosophy.

This document does not define workflows, business rules, field sets, screen behavior, data structures, numbering schemes, or technical architecture.

Where a necessary Owner decision does not yet exist, this document leaves it explicitly unresolved instead of inventing content.

## 3. Product Nature

Ard Kanaan is a simple financial operating system for an educational center.

## 4. Product Exclusions

Ard Kanaan is not:

- ERP
- Accounting software
- Student Management System
- School Management System
- CRM
- Reporting platform

## 5. Operational Areas

The product currently consists of only four operational areas:

1. Receipt Vouchers
2. Student Statement
3. Payment Vouchers
4. Financial Report

Financial Reports exist only to present the financial information generated by the product.

Ard Kanaan is not a general-purpose reporting platform, analytics platform, business intelligence system, or decision-support system.

## 6. Central Entity And Financial Truth

The student is the central business entity.

The voucher is the sole financial source of truth.

## 7. Voucher Principles

Every financial movement must have a voucher.

Every voucher represents exactly one financial event.

Receipt Vouchers create incoming financial facts.

Payment Vouchers create outgoing financial facts.

## 8. Student Statement Principles

Student Statements are generated automatically.

They are never edited manually.

Student Statements are chronological.

A new course creates a new financial obligation inside the student's existing statement.

It never creates a new student statement.

## 9. Student Identity Principles

Students are created automatically during their first financial transaction.

Future vouchers always reuse the same student.

## 10. Course Boundary

The course exists only as financial context.

The product does not manage academic courses.

## 11. Human Input And System Calculation

Users enter facts.

The system derives results.

Calculated values must never be entered manually.

## 12. Storage Boundary

The system stores facts.

The system never stores derived balances as truth.

## 13. Correction Principle

Corrections happen by correcting vouchers.

Never by editing statements.

## 14. Traceability Principle

Every financial value displayed by the system must always be traceable back to its originating voucher.

## 15. Simplicity Boundary

Simplicity has priority over features.

If a feature increases complexity without directly supporting the four operational areas, it is outside product scope unless explicitly approved by the Owner.

## 16. Unresolved Owner Decisions

None.

Any detail not stated here belongs in future constitutional or specification documents unless the Owner explicitly raises it to first-principles status.
